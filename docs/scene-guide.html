Scene Guide

Use this page when editing the story.

Each scene has markup in index.html, styling in src/styles/main.css, and animation timing in src/scripts/main.js.

Scene 1 - Title Intro

Purpose: introduces the title and sets the cinematic window/camera metaphor.

  • Markup: .scene-1
  • Key assets: window_frame.png, iceberg_bg.jpg
  • Animation area: intro autoplay timeline and early master timeline.

Scene 2 - The Problem

Purpose: reframes climate change from something distant to something personal.

  • Markup: .scene-2
  • Key idea: text is split with SplitType and revealed in sequence.

Scene 3 - Effects

Purpose: shows warming, melting ice, heatwaves, droughts, floods, storms, and ecosystem disruption.

  • Markup: .scene-3
  • Key assets: globe_space.mp4, world.svg, sun.png, tree images, storm cloud images, hurricane.gif
  • Implementation note: rain drops are generated dynamically in JavaScript.

Scene 4 - Causes

Purpose: explains human causes through a vertical timeline path.

  • Markup: .scene-4
  • Key assets: factory.png, house.png, car.png, tree images, building images
  • Implementation note: JavaScript measures the SVG path length, then animates dash offset to draw the line.

Scene 5 - What We Can Do

Purpose: shifts the story from problem to action.

  • Markup: .scene-5
  • Enter key unlocks the transition from 5A to 5B.
  • Energy usage buttons reveal subtext.
  • Key assets: bus.png, walk.png, bicycle.png, bottle.png, recycle_bag.png, recycle.png, handshake.png

Scene 6 - Call To Action

Purpose: ends with a hopeful final message and CTA button.

  • Markup: .scene-6
  • Key assets: healthy_forest.jpeg, kemarau.jpg
  • The final US word is highlighted with .s6-us-highlight.
  • The CTA button uses .cta-button::before to draw the border on hover.
