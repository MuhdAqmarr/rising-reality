Climate change is no longer just a distant headline.

Its effects are becoming more visible across the world.

The earth is getting warmer

Global temperatures are rising year by year.

As temperatures rise, ice
begins to melt.

Glaciers shrink, and more
water flows into the oceans.

Over time, sea levels rise, increasing the risk of coastal flooding.

These changes do not happen alone.

They affect the way our climate behaves.

HEATWAVES

Higher temperatures make
heatwaves more intense,
frequent, and longer-lasting.

DROUGHTS & WILDFIRES

More heat increases evaporation.

Land becomes drier, creating conditions
for droughts and wildfires.

HEAVY RAIN & FLOODS

A warmer atmosphere holds
more moisture.

This leads to heavier rainfall and increased flooding.

STORMS & HURRICANES

Warmer oceans provide more
energy to storms.

This can make them more powerful and destructive.

DISRUPTION OF ECOSYSTEMS

Rapid environmental changes outpace
the ability of many species to adapt.

Forests shrink, habitats disappear,
and some species face extinction.

What once seemed like distant events are becoming part of our shared reality.

Climate change does not happen on its own.

It is driven by human activities.

EMISSIONS

This releases gases into the air that
trap heat in the atmosphere.

We burn fossil fuels like coal, oil,
and gas to power our homes, cars, and industries.

DEFORESTATION

Forests are cleared for development.

Fewer trees mean less carbon is absorbed.

URBAN & INDUSTRIAL GROWTH

Rapid urbanization and industrial activities

increase energy demand and emissions.
